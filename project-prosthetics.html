Custom Finger Prosthetics

Specialized ergonomic solutions for scleroderma-related amputations.

Why

The primary goal was to restore writing ability and daily functional usability for a specific patient, focusing on long-term comfort and a perfect fit.

How

Modeled in SolidWorks using 3D scans of the patient's hand. Designed with specialized tool inserts and lockable rotational points.

Specs

Custom-fitted ergonomic socket. Optimised for fine motor tasks like writing and holding specialized instruments.

Patient-Centric Design

Engineering for the human body requires a unique set of constraints—specifically, the need for extreme ergonomic precision. Unlike purely mechanical projects, these prosthetics had to account for soft tissue compression and variable pressure points to avoid sores during all-day wear.

Using 3D scanning and parametric modeling in SolidWorks, I developed a modular socket system. This allowed the user to swap out "tips" for different tasks. For example, one tip was optimized with a specific angle for holding a pen, while others featured lockable rotation to assist with gripping objects in specialized orientations.
