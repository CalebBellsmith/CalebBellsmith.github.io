Custom Finger Prosthetics

Precision ergonomics for daily functional restoration.

Why

To restore writing ability and usability for a patient with amputations resulting from scleroderma.

How

Modeled in SolidWorks with ergonomic specialized tool inserts and lockable rotation for daily tasks.

Specs

Custom-fitted and optimized for specific tasks like writing or locking in specialized orientations.

Patient-Centric Design

The challenge was ergonomic fit. Unlike standard prosthetics, these needed to be comfortable for all-day wear while allowing for modular attachments. I utilized 3D scanning to ensure the socket fit was perfect, reducing pressure points while maintaining enough grip for the user to write clearly for the first time in years.
