Technical Design & SolidWorks Gallery

A collection of complex surface models and mechanical assemblies.

Core Competencies

Parametric Design • Motion Simulation • Photorealistic Rendering • Technical Drawings

Mastering the Digital Workbench

Beyond functional prototyping, I use SolidWorks to push the boundaries of complex geometry. This gallery showcases my ability to transition between mechanical precision—such as assembly motion and tolerances—and organic surfacing exercises like the jet turbine and floral geometry.

My workflow focuses on **Parametric Design**, ensuring that models are built with a robust feature tree that allows for rapid iteration. By simulating motion and stress within the CAD environment, I can identify mechanical failures before they ever reach a 3D printer or CNC machine.
