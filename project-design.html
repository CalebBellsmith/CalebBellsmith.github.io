Why

These projects combined my love for creation with particular areas of my interest. The various trees and wolf representing a love for nature, while the rings and cross show my love for metal working for my core beliefs.

How

The larger dark tree is handcarved basswood. The norse style tree is a hand woodburnt artpiece on pine. Finally, the rings are woven copper, sealed with solder. The cross is railway spikes welded and ground down to be visually appealing.

Specs

The carving is 3.5 feet tall and 2.5 feet wide. All projects were done personally by hand. The cross was MIG welded.
