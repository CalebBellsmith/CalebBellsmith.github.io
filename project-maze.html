CALEB BELLSMITH

Tilting Maze Platform

Why

To master rotational actuation and bridge the gap between mechanical design and software.

How

3D printed hardware controlled by an ESP32 and high-torque servos with custom circuitry.

Specs

Dual-axis tilting platform with modular maze inserts and planned Computer Vision integration.

Mechatronics & Control

The core challenge of this project was smooth motion control. I used an ESP32 to process joystick inputs and map them to servo angles. I am currently working on a computer vision layer using a webcam to track the ball's position, allowing the platform to tilt autonomously and solve the maze without human input.
