Tilting Maze Platform

A study in rotational actuation and embedded control systems.

Why

To master rotational actuation and bridge the gap between mechanical design and real-time software control.

How

3D printed hardware controlled by an ESP32 and high-torque servos with custom-built circuitry.

Specs

Dual-axis tilting platform with modular maze inserts and planned Computer Vision integration.

Mechatronics & Control

The core challenge of this project was achieving smooth motion control. I utilized an ESP32 to process joystick inputs and map them to servo angles. The hardware includes a modular plate system, allowing different maze designs to be hot-swapped onto the tilting platform.

I am currently developing a computer vision layer using a webcam to track the ball's position. This will allow the ESP32 to tilt the platform autonomously, solving the maze through real-time coordinate feedback without human intervention.
