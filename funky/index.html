018×8 ambient lamp

An ESP32 driving 64 WS2812B LEDs with eight generative effects (ocean, lava, pool caustics, aurora, fireflies, candle, rain, music), scenes, a sleep timer and a sunrise alarm. The panel below is the same one as the Mac app, running entirely in the browser: without a board it simulates the effects live, and in Chrome, Edge or Arc it connects to the real board over Bluetooth.

Stark Sight

01Gesture control through a webcam

MediaPipe tracks both hands and the upper body; three classifiers (geometric rules, Google's gesture model and a pose network I trained on 1.3M hands) vote on every frame, and the winner becomes real keyboard and mouse input. It runs locally on the Mac, because a web page isn't allowed to move your cursor, so this tab launches it and shows its live view.

mtsimMicroLED mass-transfer line simulator

PythonMonte CarloDiscrete-event simSobol sensitivity

Compares four ways of building microLED displays (cartridge-based two-step transfer, elastomer stamp, laser transfer and pick-and-place) by following every subpixel back to the wafer device that landed on it, then running the whole factory as a discrete-event simulation and sweeping the parameters nobody publishes.

  • 99% busy: cartridge loading is the bottleneck while the print station starves 76% of the time
  • 55× fewer repairs than an elastomer stamp line for a 12.3" automotive panel
  • 9 vs 36 optimal colour bins, depending on whether sorting happens during loading or off-line

All numbers not marked as vendor claims are illustrative assumptions of the model, not data from any company.

meetingMeeting Cost

Desktop app (PySide6)Rebuilt for the browser

A stopwatch that counts money instead of minutes. Add who's in the room, hit start, and watch the meeting's cost climb at everyone's combined salary rate (yearly salary ÷ 2,080 hours). People can come and go mid-meeting without rewriting what's already been spent. The desktop version encrypts its salary list; this one keeps it in your browser only.

joltJolt dashboard

ReactTypeScriptWeb BluetoothHack Canada 2025

The companion web app for Jolt, a forearm band that lets upper-limb amputees move a cursor by tilting their arm. It pairs with the band over Web Bluetooth and shows connection and power state, live gyroscope readings and a live view of the arm's movement, so sensitivity and EMG placement can be tuned without touching firmware.
