CALEB BELLSMITH

Custom Cedar Strip Canoe

Why

To blend a passion for the outdoors with high-level digital fabrication and craftsmanship.

How

Used CAD for hull design and CNC-machined molds to ensure 100% accuracy over 17 feet.

Specs

17-foot classic cedar strip construction with custom-made components and marine epoxy.

Precision Woodworking

This project was a test of patience and precision engineering. By using CNC stations, I ensured the hull was perfectly symmetrical. The most challenging aspect was the "bead and cove" joinery, where hundreds of cedar strips had to be hand-aligned to create a seamless, watertight shell before applying the protective fiberglass layer.
