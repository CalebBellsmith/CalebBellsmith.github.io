CALEB BELLSMITH

Custom Cedar Strip Canoe

Why

I’ve always loved the outdoors and wanted to blend that passion with advancing design concepts. Building this canoe allowed for full customization of every detail.

How

We utilized CAD and 3D modeling to perfect the molds. Then, a CNC machine was used for the secondary molds to ensure high accuracy.

Specs

A classic 17-foot cedar strip canoe where every component was custom-made for a clean, professional finish.

Engineering the Hull

The project began with a digital hull design to ensure hydrodynamics and stability. By using CNC-machined stations, I was able to maintain tolerances within a fraction of an inch across the 17-foot span. The strips were hand-planed to create "bead and cove" joins, ensuring a seamless, watertight fit before the application of fiberglass and marine-grade epoxy.
