Custom Cedar Strip Canoe

17-foot custom-built vessel blending CNC precision with traditional craft.

Why

I wanted to blend my passion for the outdoors with advanced engineering concepts. Building this canoe allowed for total customization of every structural and aesthetic detail.

How

The hull was designed in CAD to ensure hydrodynamics. I utilized CNC-machined secondary molds to maintain strict tolerances across the entire 17-foot span.

Specs

17-foot classic cedar strip construction. Features bead-and-cove joinery, fiberglass reinforcement, and marine-grade epoxy finish.

The Engineering Journey

This project was a study in precision at scale. Traditional canoe building relies heavily on manual measurement, but to ensure a perfectly symmetrical hull, I integrated digital fabrication into the process.

The most challenging phase involved the "bead and cove" joinery. Each individual cedar strip was hand-planed to ensure a seamless fit, preventing gaps that could compromise the structural integrity of the fiberglass bond. The result is a vessel that is as much a piece of engineering as it is a functional outdoor tool.
