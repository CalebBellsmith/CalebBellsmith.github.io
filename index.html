About

I am a first-year Biomedical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. My interests are centered on the intersection of mechanical design and healthcare innovation, as well as innovating on simple daily products. I truly love the end-to-end engineering process, with a particular focus on mechanical assemblies and physical creation. From initial CAD modeling to hardware fabrication and circuits, I strive to create legitimately applicable designs for real-world engineering challenges.
