Custom Cedar Strip Canoe

Designed and built a custom 17-foot cedar strip canoe combining traditional woodworking with modern digital fabrication.

Why: I wanted to blend my love for the outdoors with advanced design tools. Building my own canoe let me tailor every detail while experimenting with CNC machining and 3D modeling.

How: Used CAD and 3D modeling to design the molds, then CNC-machined secondary molds for better accuracy. Each cedar plank was milled from scratch for strength and fit.

Specs: 17-foot classic cedar strip build. Every part was custom-made for a clean, unified finish.

Orthotic Hand Exoskeleton

Developed a fully mechanical, 3D-printed orthotic device to help people with limited upper-limb mobility.

Why: Built for users with weak forearm strength or poor wrist mobility. The design shifts load from the hand and forearm through a simple mechanical system.

How: Created dozens of 3D-printed prototypes to find the most effective general design. Regular feedback guided each iteration to improve comfort and function.

Specs: 100% 3D-printed and purely mechanical. The design is scalable and customizable—users can choose specific fingers for support.

Custom Finger Prosthetics

Created 3D-modeled and printed prosthetic fingers for a patient with multiple amputations on their dominant hand.

Why: The goal was to help the patient regain writing ability. Each prosthetic was fitted precisely for comfort and function.

How: Modeled and prototyped multiple designs, refining ergonomic features and adding specialized tools like lockable rotation and writing inserts.

Specs: Each piece is custom-fitted, optimized for comfort, and tailored to specific actions like writing or rotation.
