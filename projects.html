Custom Cedar Strip Canoe

I designed and built a 17-foot cedar strip canoe that mixes old-school woodworking with modern tech.

Why: I’ve always loved the outdoors so I wanted to blend that passion with advancing design concepts Building this canoe with my team allowed us to customize every tiny detail while getting hands-on with CNC machining and 3D modeling.

How: We started with CAD and 3D modeling to get the molds (which the canoe is then built off of) perfect. Then, we used a CNC machine for the secondary molds for high accurate. Every single cedar plank was milled from scratch to make sure the fit was tight and the adhesive bonded correctly.

Specs: A classic 17-foot ceder strip canoe where every piece was custom-made for a clean, professional finish.

Orthotic Hand Exoskeleton

This is a fully mechanical, 3D-printed device I made to help people who have trouble moving their arms or hands.

Why: This design was created in order to assist individuals with minimal forearm, hand, and wrist strength. The idea was to create a mechanical system that shifts the heavy lifting away from the hand and forearm.

How: To achieve this I went through dozens of 3D-printed prototypes to figure out the optimal design for fitment and strength. I used constant user feedback to tweak the comfort and make sure it functioned ideally.

Specs: It's 100% 3D-printed and purely mechanical. It’s also scalable, so users can pick exactly which fingers they need support for.

Custom Finger Prosthetics

I used 3D modeling and printing to create custom prosthetic fingers for a patient who had amputations on their dominant hand due to scleroderma.

Why: The main goal of this project was to help someone get their writing ability back as well as increase the usability of all their fingers. I wanted to make sure each prosthetic was fitted perfectly so it felt comfortable while they used it.

How: In order to account for different uses of the finger, I modelled a variety of different designs. I focused on making them ergonomic and even added some specialized features, like rotation that you can lock and inserts specifically for holding a pen.

Specs: Custom-fitted for the best comfort and tailored for specific tasks like writing or locking in a given orientation.
