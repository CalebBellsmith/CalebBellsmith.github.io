Hand Exoskeletons

Biomechatronics for upper-limb mobility assistance.

Why

Assisting mobility by shifting loads away from small joints.

How

3D-printed linkages integrated with EMG sensor feedback.

Specs

Scalable, modular design with tool-less adjustability.

Prototyping Mobility

This project involved dozens of 3D-printed iterations to balance grip force with lightweight wearability. The EMG integration allows the device to amplify the user's natural intent into functional mechanical force.
