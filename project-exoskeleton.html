Hand Exoskeletons

Restoring upper-limb mobility through biomechatronics.

Why

Assisting individuals with limited strength by shifting lifting loads away from the hand via mechanical linkages.

How

Rapid prototyping with 3D printing. The EMG version uses sensors to process neuromuscular signals via ESP32.

Specs

Fully 3D-printed and scalable. Featuring tool-less adjustability and signal-based movement.

Signal Processing & Prototyping

Iterative design was key here. I went through dozens of failures to find the right balance between joint mobility and structural rigidity. By integrating EMG (electromyography) sensors, the device can read small muscle twitches in the forearm and amplify that intent into mechanical grip strength, providing a life-changing boost in mobility for the user.
