Biomechatronics Hand Exoskeletons

Why

These designs were created to assist individuals with minimal strength by shifting heavy lifting away from the hand through mechanical linkages.

How

I developed dozens of 3D-printed prototypes for optimal strength. The EMG version utilizes sensors to detect neuromuscular signals processed by an ESP32.

Specs

100% 3D-printed and scalable. Features tool-less adjustability and intuitive signal-based movement.

Signal Processing & Prototyping

Iterative design was key here. I went through dozens of failures to find the right balance between joint mobility and structural rigidity. By integrating EMG (electromyography) sensors, the device can read small muscle twitches in the forearm and amplify that intent into mechanical grip strength, providing a life-changing boost in mobility for the user.
